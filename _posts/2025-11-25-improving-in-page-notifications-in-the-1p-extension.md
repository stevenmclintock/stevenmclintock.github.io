Working at 1Password for nearly four years, most of my work has focused on foundational improvements--important changes that users don’t always see directly.

This summer, I had the chance to team up with [Hannah Hwong](https://hannahhwong.github.io/portfolio/) on something much more visible: improving the notifications experience in the 1Password browser extension, one of the features our users interact with the most.

I’m excited to share that after a smooth rollout to our Nightly and Beta channels, we’re beginning to launch these improvements to all of our stable users!

If you’d like to learn more about what we built and why, check out the full write-up at [Improving in-page notifications in the 1Password browser extension](https://1password.com/blog/improving-in-page-notifications-in-the-1password-browser-extension)!
